What we've heard

What we've heard

  • You need a scalable way to educate suppliers and pharma requesters so the team stops repeating one-to-one training and answering the same onboarding and billing questions.
  • Roughly 300 to 350 monthly tickets and about 24 hours a week of account manager training time are the load you want to reclaim.
  • A 21-day onboarding timeline is the ideal for getting a new account to first value.
  • You want the full picture of the training ecosystem for requesters, suppliers and employees in one POC.
  • Your customer-facing AI assistant should become a primary way people reach learning content, without losing any of its current capabilities.
  • The hackathon starts Oct 12 and you want to show a working, branded academy built with your own video and HTML content.
  • Leadership wants data tied back to Salesforce and business outcomes, not only completion counts.
  • You want a real implementation, customer success and support team behind you, not one person reinventing the wheel.

Faster supplier activation and onboarding toward the 21-day target, with fewer repeat onboarding and billing tickets.

Self-service becomes the front door to learning and deflects tickets before they reach your team.
Report learner activity, completion and drop-off, and confirm the path to send it to Salesforce Leadership wants more than completion counts, including abandonment and session depth tied to business outcomes. Evidence linking education to tickets, onboarding time and transaction growth for the leadership business case.
Confirm learners can sign in through Science Exchange SSO Suppliers and requesters need a single login, and the SSO setup has not yet been validated. Removes sign-in friction for the audiences that drive activation and confirms technical fit.
